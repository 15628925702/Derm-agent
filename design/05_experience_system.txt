[Experience System]

Definition:
Experience = Structured reasoning knowledge from past cases.

Types:

1. raw_case_experience
2. hard_case_experience
3. confusion_experience
4. prototype_experience

Schema:

{
  case_id,
  perception_summary,
  skills_used,
  key_evidence,
  error_type,
  confusion_pair,
  learning_points
}

Rules:

- Only meaningful cases are stored
- Confusion cases prioritized
- Supports retrieval before reasoning
